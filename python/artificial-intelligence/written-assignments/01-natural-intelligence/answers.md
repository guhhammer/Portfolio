# Class 01 – Natural intelligence (discussion questions)

Artificial Intelligence course, PUCPR, 2021. Author: Gustavo Hammerschmidt.
Team: André de Macedo Wlodkovski, Gustavo Hammerschmidt, Isa Stohler Bertolaccini.
Each answer is followed by whether the team reached a consensus. The original answers (in Portuguese) are in `answers.docx`.

**1) To what extent is intelligence an attribute unique to the human species?**

The intelligence we know is unique to the human species, judging by the number of neurons and the complexity of the genome. There may be intelligences shaped differently from ours that we fail to recognise as intelligence when we judge them by comparison with our own.

Yes, the team agrees.

**2) Can a machine be designed that displays intelligent behaviour?**

Yes. Intelligence can emerge from behaviours of high hierarchy and logical complexity.

Yes, the team agrees.

**3) Can man create a machine more intelligent than himself?**

No. He would need an equivalent level of understanding in order to understand it.

No, the team disagrees on this one.

**4) Are there components of human intelligence that cannot be found in any other animal or duplicated in a machine?**

Yes, abilities such as shape recognition, the interpretation of natural signs, and so on.

Yes, the team agrees.

**5) Can intelligent behaviour be duplicated by decomposing a problem into a sequence of sub-problems?**

It would depend on the randomness of the processes and on the level of entropy. The more complex a problem is, the less likely it is that the means to the end will be the same.

No, the team disagrees on this one.

**6) Is human intelligence limited to human experience, or does it have hereditary components?**

If, at some point, there was a common ancestor between species, then certain behaviours can be inherited; however, some may evolve and others fall out of use and be forgotten.

Yes, the team agrees.

**7) Is intelligent behaviour a property of organic structure?**

No. Intelligence is not imprinted in organic matter; it is an effect of the communication between matter, consciousness being pure energy.

Yes, the team agrees.

**8) Is human intelligence cumulative? In what sense?**

Yes, in the sense that it intensifies repeated processes so as to perform similar activities with less energy.

Yes, the team agrees.
