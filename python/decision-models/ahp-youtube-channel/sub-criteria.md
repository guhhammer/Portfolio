# AHP sub-criteria (share of channel views)

Thresholds used for the five criteria of the YouTube-channel AHP model (see `paper.tex` / `report.pdf`).

| Criterion | Sub-criterion | Range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C1 | SC 1.1 | above 70% of views |
| C1 | SC 1.2 | between 40% and 70% of views |
| C1 | SC 1.3 | below 40% of views |
| C2 | SC 2.1 | above 80% of views |
| C2 | SC 2.2 | from 70% to 80% of views |
| C2 | SC 2.3 | below 70% of views |
| C3 | SC 3.1 | above 10% of views |
| C3 | SC 3.2 | from 2% to 10% of views |
| C3 | SC 3.3 | below 2% of views |
| C4 | SC 4.1 | above 40% of views |
| C4 | SC 4.2 | from 10% to 40% of views |
| C4 | SC 4.3 | below 10% of views |
| C5 | SC 5.1 | above 90% of views |
| C5 | SC 5.2 | from 40% to 90% of views |
| C5 | SC 5.3 | below 40% of views |
