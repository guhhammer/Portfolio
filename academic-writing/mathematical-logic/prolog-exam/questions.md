# Exam questions (translated)

1. Given a family relation with facts about who is married to whom and who is
   whose mother, build a relation called *concunhado* (co-sibling-in-law).
   Answer: `family-in-laws.pl`.

2. A restaurant can identify a list of customers, a list of starters, main
   courses and desserts, and a list of wine types served during the meals.
   Every customer has meal and wine preferences. Build a system that, given a
   piece of information about the wine type, the dessert, the main course or
   the starter, provides the other pieces of information.
   Answer: `restaurant-preferences.pl`.
