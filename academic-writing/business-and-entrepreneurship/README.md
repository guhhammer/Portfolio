# Business and entrepreneurship

The "Creative Experience" course at PUCPR (2020): a startup idea taken from ideation to a pitch over thirteen classes. My team's idea was **Ingredient** (later branded **Ruptify**), a community recipe app that connects recipes to local organic-produce suppliers. Documents are in Portuguese; the folders follow the course weeks.

| Folder | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `ingredient-startup/` | The pitch deck, its script, the original idea note and the two logos. |
| `class-01-ideation/` | Notes on innovations discussed in the first class. |
| `class-02-brainwriting/` | The brainwriting board (CSV export and image). |
| `class-03-assignment-2/` | Assignment 2 write-up and the first two Ingredient activity sheets. |
| `class-04-personas-and-canvas/` | Persona planning document, the persona and the model canvas. |
| `class-05-management-and-budget/` | Management and budget plan (docx + pdf) with the management canvas. |
| `class-06-functional-prototype/`, `class-09-prototype-photos/` | Photos of the functional and paper prototypes. |
| `class-07-value-proposition/` | Value-proposition canvas. |
| `class-08-prototyping/` | The prototype board (screens and comic-strip scenarios). |
| `class-10-answers/` | Written answers to the class questions. |
| `class-12-usability/` | Usability evaluation of the prototype (docx + pdf). |
| `class-13-business-model-canvas/` | Business model canvas, filled and empty, with the write-up. |
| `assignment-3-prototype-interviews/` | Assignment 3: prototype testing with real users, including the interview questions, the form responses (CSV and spreadsheet) and screenshots of the tests. |
