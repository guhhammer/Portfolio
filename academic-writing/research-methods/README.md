# Research methods

Course on research methodology for computer science (PUCPR, 2021): systematic literature reviews, surveys, grounded theory, experiments and how to write them up. Documents are in Portuguese; the three main deliverables were written in LaTeX (sources for the survey in [`latex-projects/`](../latex-projects/)).

| Folder | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `systematic-literature-review/` | A systematic review protocol and results (`systematic-review.pdf`), the defence presentation, an optional rewrite and the summary of the review used as a model. |
| `survey/` | A survey on knowledge of the state of the art in AI (`survey-project.pdf`) with the response charts in `figures/`, the presentation, and a second survey instrument on AI in journalism. |
| `grounded-theory/` | A grounded-theory study (`grounded-theory.pdf`): open coding spreadsheet, axial coding diagram, category maps in `figures/`, the propositions, the team presentation and the preparatory activity 13. |
| `activities/` | The short in-class activities (research problems, methods, search strings, planning templates filled in, evaluation rubric). |
| `assignments/` | Assignments 2 and 3 (the review summary that was assignment 1 is in the systematic-review folder). |
