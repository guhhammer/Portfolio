# MATLAB / Octave

Applied probability and statistics coursework written in MATLAB and GNU Octave. The scripts are translated to English and every one of them was executed in Octave 9 (functions that rely on the Statistics toolbox are marked as such).

| Folder | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [`computational-modelling/`](computational-modelling/) | Computational Modelling course (PUCPR, 2019): anomaly detection with a multivariate Gaussian, Monte Carlo simulations against closed-form answers, random-variable generators, a naive Bayes classifier, ordinary least squares, and the team worksheets and spreadsheets produced during the course. |
